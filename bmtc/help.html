You found the seminar your facilitator is opening and applied to it yourself — your name shows as Pending until the facilitator approves it.Ulipata semina ambayo mwezeshaji wako anafungua na kuiomba mwenyewe — jina lako linaonekana Linasubiri hadi mwezeshaji aidhinishe.

StepsHatua

  1. Academy → "Available Seminars" (the card says "Browse and apply for seminars"). It opens on your own country. Each row shows the facilitator — find your facilitator's name
  2. Find the one your facilitator named (book · place · date). Tap it → "Apply" → confirm
  3. Academy → "My Courses": it is there as Pending. After the facilitator approves it (in the seminar room) it says Approved
  4. From then on, attendance and grades for you land under this seminar
  1. Akademia → "Semina Zinazopatikana" (kadi inasema "Tafuta na uombe semina"). Inafunguka na nchi yako. Kila mstari unaonyesha mwezeshaji — tafuta jina la mwezeshaji wako
  2. Tafuta ile mwezeshaji wako aliyotaja (kitabu · mahali · tarehe). Igusa → "Omba" → thibitisha
  3. Akademia → "Kozi Zangu": iko kama Inasubiri. Mwezeshaji akiidhinisha (ndani ya chumba cha semina) inasema Imeidhinishwa
  4. Kuanzia hapo, mahudhurio na alama zako huingia chini ya semina hii

⚠️ Watch outAngalia

  • Cannot find the seminar? It may not be open for enrolment yet — ask the facilitator, do not apply to a different one
  • Applying twice is blocked; a Pending application is enough
  • After applying, the button says "Applied — waiting for approval" — that is Pending until the facilitator approves
  • Huoni semina? Labda haijafunguliwa kwa usajili — muulize mwezeshaji, usiombe semina nyingine
  • Kuomba mara mbili kumezuiwa; ombi moja Linalosubiri linatosha
  • Baada ya kuomba, kitufe kinasema "Umeomba — inasubiri idhini" — bado Inasubiri hadi mwezeshaji aidhinishe

Student: my courses — fees and gradesMwanafunzi: kozi zangu — ada na alama5 mindakika 5

You know where your own record lives: which seminars you took, which you completed, and the grade you got — the same numbers the diploma will use.Unajua kumbukumbu yako iko wapi: semina ulizochukua, ulizokamilisha, na alama uliyopata — namba zile zile cheti kitatumia.

StepsHatua

  1. Academy → "My Courses": every seminar you are in, with its status (Pending · Approved · Completed)
  2. Applied to the wrong seminar? While it is still Pending: tap "Cancel Application" on its card. You can apply again later
  3. Tap the fee badge on your seminar (for example "Due …" or "Paid in full"): your payments with receipt numbers 🧾. Amount right? Tap "Yes, I paid this". Wrong? Before the seminar ends, tell your facilitator. After it ends, ask your coordinator. Tap the seminar itself to open its room: "Grades & attendance" · "Fees" · "Exams"
  4. "Grades & attendance" in the same room: score and letter grade once the seminar is finalized. Empty until then
  5. Something wrong (a seminar missing, a grade you do not recognise)? First check the name at the top right is you. Then tell your facilitator — they fix it in the seminar room. You cannot edit here, on purpose
  6. Find it again any time: ☰ → My Courses. It is what you show when someone asks what you have studied
  1. Akademia → "Kozi Zangu": kila semina uliyo ndani, na hali yake (Inasubiri · Imeidhinishwa · Amekamilisha)
  2. Umeomba semina isiyo sahihi? Ikiwa bado Inasubiri: gusa "Ghairi Ombi" kwenye kadi yake. Unaweza kuomba tena baadaye
  3. Gusa beji ya ada kwenye semina yako (mf. "Deni …" au "Imelipwa kamili"): malipo yako na namba za risiti 🧾. Kiasi ni sahihi? Gusa "Ndiyo, nimelipa hii". Si sahihi? Kabla semina haijaisha, mwambie mwezeshaji wako. Ikiisha, muulize mratibu wako. Gusa semina yenyewe kufungua chumba chake: "Alama na mahudhurio" · "Ada" · "Mitihani"
  4. "Alama na mahudhurio" katika chumba hicho hicho: alama na daraja semina ikishamalizwa. Tupu hadi hapo
  5. Kuna kosa (semina haipo, alama usiyoitambua)? Kwanza angalia jina juu kulia ni wewe. Kisha mwambie mwezeshaji wako — anarekebisha kwenye chumba cha semina. Huwezi kuhariri hapa, kwa makusudi
  6. Kipate tena wakati wowote: ☰ → Kozi Zangu. Ndicho unachoonyesha mtu anapouliza umesoma nini

⚠️ Watch outAngalia

  • No grade yet is normal while the seminar is Ongoing — grades come with finalize
  • Kukosa alama ni kawaida semina ikiwa Inaendelea — alama huja pamoja na kumaliza

Student: ask to read the online textbookMwanafunzi: omba kusoma kitabu mtandaoni8 mindakika 8

You asked for your seminar's textbook from the bookshelf and know that a coordinator, not the facilitator, opens it — and why.Uliomba kitabu cha semina yako kutoka rafuni na unajua kwamba mratibu, si mwezeshaji, ndiye anayefungua — na kwa nini.

StepsHatua

  1. "Online Textbooks" (bookshelf): the covers you can read are on top, locked ones below. Type your course name in the search box to find your seminar's book
  2. Tap the locked cover → the book page → "Request access from administrator" → reason (I am attending this seminar) + how you will pay → "Submit request"
  3. The book's page now shows "Request pending" (the cover on the shelf still shows a lock). A coordinator — not the facilitator — approves after payment (or as support)
  4. When it opens → "Start Studying". Your answers and underlines are saved to your account
  1. "Vitabu vya Kiada Mtandaoni" (rafu): vitabu unavyoweza kusoma viko juu, vilivyofungwa chini. Andika jina la kozi yako kwenye kisanduku cha kutafuta kupata kitabu cha semina yako
  2. Gusa jalada lililofungwa → ukurasa wa kitabu → "Omba ruhusa kwa msimamizi" → sababu (nahudhuria semina hii) + utakavyolipa → "Tuma ombi"
  3. Ukurasa wa kitabu sasa unaonyesha "Ombi linasubiri" (jalada kwenye rafu bado lina kufuli). Mratibu — si mwezeshaji — anaidhinisha baada ya malipo (au kama msaada)
  4. Likifunguka → "Anza kusoma". Majibu na mistari yako huhifadhiwa kwenye akaunti yako

⚠️ Watch outAngalia

  • The exam does not need the book — you can sit it even while the cover is still locked
  • There are no Swahili online textbooks yet — the book and the exam are the English edition
  • Mtihani hauhitaji kitabu — unaweza kuufanya hata jalada likiwa bado limefungwa
  • Bado hakuna vitabu vya mtandaoni vya Kiswahili — kitabu na mtihani ni toleo la Kiingereza

Student: sit the online unit examMwanafunzi: fanya mtihani wa kitengo mtandaoni10 mindakika 10

You sat one unit exam on your phone and submitted it — and you know the score comes back through your facilitator into your seminar room.Ulifanya mtihani mmoja wa kitengo kwenye simu yako na kuuwasilisha — na unajua alama hurudi kupitia mwezeshaji wako hadi chumba chako cha semina.

StepsHatua

  1. Academy → "My Courses" → your seminar → "Exams" (or tap "Open" on this card): exams your facilitator has opened. Nothing there means no exam is open yet
  2. "Start exam" → the start time is recorded and the questions appear. If there is a time limit, it shows at the top
  3. Answer. Each answer saves as you go — a dropped connection does not lose it. Do not leave the page to look things up: leaving is recorded
  4. "Submit" at the end. It locks. The facilitator grades it; the score appears in your seminar room under "Grades & attendance" after the seminar is finalized
  1. Akademia → "Kozi Zangu" → semina yako → "Mitihani" (au gusa "Fungua" kwenye kadi hii): mitihani ambayo mwezeshaji wako amefungua. Hakuna kitu inamaanisha bado hakuna mtihani uliofunguliwa
  2. "Anza mtihani" → muda wa kuanza hurekodiwa na maswali yanatokea. Kukiwa na kikomo cha muda, kinaonekana juu
  3. Jibu. Kila jibu huhifadhiwa unapoendelea — muunganisho ukikatika haupotei. Usiondoke kwenye ukurasa kutafuta majibu: kuondoka hurekodiwa
  4. "Wasilisha" mwishoni. Inafunga. Mwezeshaji anasahihisha; alama inatokea kwenye chumba chako cha semina chini ya "Alama na mahudhurio" baada ya semina kumalizwa

⚠️ Watch outAngalia

  • Submitted is final — check before you press
  • No 'Start exam' button? You are not an approved student of that seminar yet ("Student: apply for a seminar"), or the facilitator has not opened it
  • Internet dropped when you pressed "Submit"? A message says so — your answers are kept on this phone; tap "Submit" again
  • Questions and buttons (True/False, Clear) come in the language of the book — English for now, even on a Swahili screen
  • Kuwasilisha ni mwisho — angalia kabla ya kubonyeza
  • Hakuna kitufe cha 'Anza mtihani'? Bado si mwanafunzi aliyeidhinishwa wa semina hiyo ("Mwanafunzi: omba semina"), au mwezeshaji hajaufungua
  • Mtandao ulikatika ulipobonyeza "Wasilisha"? Ujumbe unasema hivyo — majibu yako yamehifadhiwa kwenye simu hii; gusa "Wasilisha" tena
  • Maswali na vitufe (Kweli/Si kweli, Futa) huja kwa lugha ya kitabu — Kiingereza kwa sasa, hata kwenye skrini ya Kiswahili
