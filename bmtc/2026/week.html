Seven lessons from the final testMafunzo saba kutoka jaribio la mwisho

We checked every final test seminar. These are the things many of us got wrong. They are easy to fix once you see them.Tulikagua kila semina ya jaribio la mwisho. Haya ndiyo mambo ambayo wengi wetu tulikosea. Ni rahisi kurekebisha ukishayaona.

1

Your partner already has an account.

Mwenzako tayari ana akaunti.

They apply from their own phone; you tap Approve. "+ New student" is only for people with no account. On Friday many partners became copies, and then the real person could not see their own grade.

Anaomba kwa simu yake; wewe unabonyeza Approve. "+ New student" ni kwa wasio na akaunti tu. Ijumaa wenzetu wengi walikuwa nakala, na mtu halisi hakuweza kuona alama yake.

2

Grades go in Direct Score Input.

Alama zinaingia kwenye Direct Score Input.

The exam box is one part of the grade. When 88 went into the exam box, students got A or F instead of B.

Kisanduku cha mtihani ni sehemu moja tu ya alama. 88 ilipoingia kwenye kisanduku cha mtihani, wanafunzi walipata A au F badala ya B.

3

One seminar. Stuck? Fix this one.

Semina moja. Umekwama? Rekebisha hii hii.

This week we opened 107 practice seminars. At home, every extra seminar is a seminar someone must clean up.

Wiki hii tulifungua semina 107 za mazoezi. Nyumbani, kila semina ya ziada ni semina ambayo mtu lazima aisafishe.

4

One receipt = one line.

Risiti moja = mstari mmoja.

Wrong amount? Edit the same line. A second line counts the money twice.

Kiasi kibaya? Hariri mstari ule ule. Mstari wa pili unahesabu pesa mara mbili.

5

Reverse fixes a payment. Nothing else.

Reverse inarekebisha malipo. Si kitu kingine.

Do not reverse the charge (what the student owes). Waiver is a discount, not a fix: it lowers what the student must pay.

Usirudishe deni (charge — anachodaiwa mwanafunzi). Waiver ni punguzo, si marekebisho: inapunguza anachopaswa kulipa mwanafunzi.

6

A student who stops: no money, then Dropped out.

Mwanafunzi anayeacha: hakuna pesa, kisha Dropped out.

If you write money for them and reverse it, the system cannot cancel their charge by itself.

Ukiandika pesa kwake kisha ukairudisha, mfumo hauwezi kufuta deni lake wenyewe.

7

Start, then Finalize.

Start, kisha Finalize.

Start seminar closes enrollment when everyone is in. Finalize on the last day.

Start seminar inafunga usajili wote wakishaingia. Finalize siku ya mwisho.

Promise 0 and the five promisesAhadi 0 na ahadi tano

  • 0. "Record it!" It is fine to be wrong. An administrator checks and corrects. The only mistake is not recording.0. "Record it!" Ni sawa kukosea. Msimamizi anakagua na kurekebisha. Kosa pekee ni kutorekodi.
  • 1. "Own login!" Every student has their own login.1. "Own login!" Kila mwanafunzi ana kuingia kwake mwenyewe.
  • 2. "ID name, right phone!" Names as on the ID card. Check every phone number.2. "ID name, right phone!" Majina kama kwenye kitambulisho. Angalia kila namba ya simu.
  • 3. "Finalize today!" A seminar is finalized the day it ends, on the phone, right there.3. "Finalize today!" Semina humalizwa siku inayoisha, kwenye simu, pale pale.
  • 4. "Money with receipt!" Money is recorded once, on the day, with its receipt.4. "Money with receipt!" Pesa hurekodiwa mara moja, siku hiyo hiyo, pamoja na risiti yake.
  • 5. "Report every month!" A month nobody reported is a month nobody heard about.5. "Report every month!" Mwezi usioripotiwa ni mwezi ambao hakuna aliyesikia habari zake.
