Promise 0 and the five promises — say them without lookingAhadi 0 na ahadi tano — zitamke bila kuangalia약속 0과 다섯 약속 — 보지 않고 말해 보기
  • 0. "Record it!" It is fine to be wrong. An administrator checks and corrects. The only mistake is not recording
  • 1. "Own login!" Every student has their own login — they sign up themselves, or the facilitator creates it beside them in the seminar room and the student writes down the password. Record-only is for someone with truly no phone, and is linked later
  • 2. "ID name, right phone!" Names as on the ID card — that is how they are printed on the diploma. Check every phone number (the country code is added for you)
  • 3. "Finalize today!" A seminar is finalized the day it ends, with the completed count — on the phone, right there. No paper ledger
  • 4. "Money with receipt!" Money is recorded once, on the day, with its receipt — a student's fee gets a receipt number, an expense gets the receipt photo. No receipt? Record it anyway and write why
  • 5. "Report every month!" The report goes in at the end of every month — a month nobody reported is a month nobody heard about
  • 0. "Record it!" Ni sawa kukosea. Msimamizi anakagua na kurekebisha. Kosa pekee ni kutorekodi
  • 1. "Own login!" Kila mwanafunzi ana kuingia kwake mwenyewe — anajisajili mwenyewe, au mwezeshaji anamfungulia kando yake kwenye Chumba cha Semina na mwanafunzi anaandika nenosiri. Rekodi tu ni kwa asiye na simu kabisa, na huunganishwa baadaye
  • 2. "ID name, right phone!" Majina kama kwenye kitambulisho — ndivyo yanavyochapishwa kwenye cheti. Angalia kila namba ya simu (msimbo wa nchi huongezwa kwa ajili yako)
  • 3. "Finalize today!" Semina humalizwa siku inayoisha, pamoja na idadi ya waliokamilisha — kwenye simu, pale pale. Hakuna daftari la karatasi
  • 4. "Money with receipt!" Pesa hurekodiwa mara moja, siku hiyo hiyo, pamoja na risiti yake — ada ya mwanafunzi inapata namba ya risiti, gharama inapata picha ya risiti. Hakuna risiti? Rekodi hata hivyo na uandike kwa nini
  • 5. "Report every month!" Ripoti huingia mwisho wa kila mwezi — mwezi usioripotiwa ni mwezi ambao hakuna aliyesikia habari zake
  • 0. 「Record it!」 틀려도 된다. 관리자가 확인하고 고친다. 안 적는 것만이 잘못이다
  • 1. 「Own login!」 학생마다 자기 로그인이 있다 — 스스로 가입하거나, 인도자가 수강실에서 옆에 앉아 만들어 주고 비밀번호는 학생이 적는다. 기록용은 폰이 정말 없는 사람용이고 나중에 연결한다
