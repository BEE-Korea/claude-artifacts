Step 3 / 12 · Students come

👥Your three students come in

The storyYour partner joins from their own phone. Two new people, F1 and F2, have no account — you make one for each.
Do
  1. Partner applies on their phone. You: seminar room → Students → Applied by themselves → Approve
  2. + New student: First name Student F1, Last name = your first name. Then the same for Student F2
  3. You as a student: Academy → Available Seminars → find your partner's name on the row → Apply
✅ Never make your partner as a new student — they already have an account. "+ New student" is only for people with no account (F1, F2).
✅ Many seminars of the same course are open today. Look at the facilitator's name before you tap Apply.
Step 4 / 12 · The online book

  2. Tap the Approved tab (it opens on Pending — F1 is not there)

Step 5 / 12 · Class starts

🔒Close the door — class starts

The storyEveryone is in. No more people can join.
Do
  1. Wait: your partner is approved in your seminar, and you have applied to your partner's seminar
  2. My Seminars → your seminar → Start seminar (close enrollment)
✅ Press it too early and your partner cannot apply. So: approve first, then press.
Step 6 / 12 · First class

✋First class — who came?

The storyToday is the first class. Your partner and F1 came on time. F2 did not come. The other classes have not happened yet.
Do — box 1 only
  1. Seminar room → Attendance → scroll to the student cards
  2. Partner: tap box 1 once → On time (green)
  3. F1: tap box 1 once → On time (green)
  4. F2: tap box 1 four times → On time → Late → Early leave → Absent
  5. Boxes 2, 3 … : do not touch
🧪 Every box says "Absent" before you tap — that only means "not marked yet". F2's four taps save the absence. It looks the same, but now it is a record.
✅ One box = one class. Mark it on the day, on the phone.

Step 8 / 12 · F2 stops

🚪F2 never came back

The storyF2 signed up, missed the first class, and never came again.
Do
  1. Seminar room → Students → F2 → Delete → Delete (red)
  2. The window asks — tap Mark as dropped out
✅ Delete = you added the wrong person by mistake. Dropped out = a real student who stopped. F2 stays in the history as Cancelled.
🧪 The window appears because of step 6 (F2's absence is a record). No window? Go back to step 6.
Step 9 / 12 · Last day

📝The seminar ends — grades and finalize

The storyThe seminar is over today. Your partner scored 88. F1 scored 74. F2 has no grade — F2 dropped out.
Do
  1. Seminar room → Grade Summary
  2. In Direct Score Input: partner 88 · F1 74 (it saves by itself; B and C appear)
  3. Leave the other boxes empty → Finalize seminar
🧪 88 and 74 are the same for everyone, so your partner can check they see B.
✅ At home: type the student's real final score — one score is enough. Finalize on the last day. Too early? A coordinator taps Undo finalize.
